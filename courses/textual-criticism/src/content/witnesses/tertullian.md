Tertullian matters for two reasons. He is an early Latin writer, and the Catholic Encyclopedia calls him "the first witness to the existence of a Latin Bible". But the same article notes that he seems often to have translated from the Greek Bible as he wrote. So a Latin quotation in Tertullian may show the Greek text he read, the Latin Bible of his church, or his own wording.
