*Written from Faithlife's help pages for Logos version 55 (November 2026). Not yet checked in the app.*

## What it does

**Text Comparison** shows one passage in several Bible versions. It compares each version with a **base text** and marks the differences. Translators use it to see the choices other translations made.

## Open it

- **Tools menu:** **Text Comparison**.
- **In a Bible:** on the **Tools** tab, click **Compare**.
- **Right-click:** choose **Selection** or **Reference** on the left, then **Text Comparison** on the right.

The base text is the Bible you used last. To change it, open the Bible you want as the base, then open Text Comparison again.

## Choose the versions

1. Click the blue version link to the right of the reference box.
2. Tick the versions you want. You can type a name in **Find**, or choose a collection such as **Top Bibles**.
3. Click **Save** to keep this set for next time. If you only click outside the list, Logos uses your choice now but forgets it later.

## See the differences

- **Show differences:** words that differ from the base text turn blue. Point at a red circle to see the base wording. Each verse ends with how much it differs, as a percentage.
- **Show base text:** shows the base wording inside each version, with words that are missing struck through.
- **Ignore case, marks or punctuation:** in the **More Actions** menu (three dots). Use it to hide small differences.
- **Interlinear:** in the **Verses / Interlinear** list. Lines up each version word by word with the base text.

Also in the More Actions menu: **Follow** (move with your Bible), vertical or horizontal layout, and print or export (in Verses view only).

## Offline

Text Comparison works offline, except for the Interlinear view, which needs the internet.

## A tip for checking

Make English and regional-language Bibles your **Top Bibles** or a collection. Then one click compares the passage in every translation your team uses.
