*Written from Faithlife's help pages for Logos version 55 (November 2026). Not yet checked in the app.*

## Go to a passage

- **Bible icon:** click the Bible icon on the main toolbar. Your preferred Bible opens at the last passage you read.
- **Command Box:** type a reference, for example `Mark 1:2`, in the Command Box at the top of the window, then choose from the list.
- **Reference box:** in an open Bible, type a new reference in the box at the top of the panel.

To make another Bible your preferred one, type `set preferred Bible` in the Command Box.

## Move through the text

Each book has a **dynamic toolbar** with tabs: Home, Formatting, Notes, View, Search, Tools and Share.

- On the **Home** tab, the navigation buttons move to the next or previous chapter or verse. The **Contents** button shows the table of contents.
- The **history arrows** next to the reference box go back and forward. Right-click an arrow to see where you have been.
- The arrow keys and the mouse wheel scroll. In **Paged View** (on the **View** tab), the Space bar turns the page.

## Find an entry in a lexicon or other book

Non-Bible books work in the same way, with two extras:

1. Click **Contents** on the Home tab. Each book orders its contents in its own way: alphabetically, by topic or by number.
2. Or click the **Find box** at the top of the panel. It shows what you can look up in this book. In a lexicon you can type a headword or a Strong's number.

To type Greek or Hebrew, choose a keyboard with the selector next to the Find box.

From a Bible, right-click a word and choose its lemma (the dictionary form). Logos looks it up in your top lexicons.

Some books show page numbers or other markers. Turn them on under **Formatting** › **Markers**.

## Make the text larger

- **One book:** on the **View** tab, use **Zoom**.
- **All books:** Program Settings › **Accessibility** › **Content Scaling**.
- **Menus and buttons too:** **Program Scaling**, in the main toolbar's More Actions menu or in Program Settings › Accessibility.
