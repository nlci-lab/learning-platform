*Written from Faithlife's help pages for Logos version 55 (November 2026). Not yet checked in the app.*

## Open the Library

Click the **Library** icon on the toolbar, or press Ctrl+L (Windows) or Cmd+L (Mac). The Library lists every book you own, including books that are only in the cloud.

Choose how it looks from the **Views** list: **Cover View**, **Tile View** or **Details View**. In Details View, click a column heading such as **Type** or **Author** to group the books.

## Find a book

- **Type** a few words of the title or author in the Library's search bar.
- **Filter:** click the **Sidebar** icon to open the filters. You can filter by **Type** (for example Bible, commentary or lexicon), subject, **Collection**, reading status or date added.
- **Field search:** type a field and a word, for example `title:John` or `author:Augustine`.

## Prioritise books

Logos opens your **prioritised** books first: in the Passage Guide and other guides, when you look up a word, and when you open a Bible link. Prioritise the Bibles, dictionaries and commentaries you trust most.

1. In the Library, right-click a book and choose **Prioritize This Book**. It goes to the bottom of the list.
2. Drag books up or down. Higher in the list means higher priority.
3. To take a book off the list, right-click it and choose **Remove from this list**.

You can only change priorities in the desktop app, but they also apply on the web and on your phone.

Prioritising does **not** change the order of results in the Search tool.

## Top Bible and Top Bibles

- Your **Top Bible** (also called your preferred Bible) opens when you click a reference or the Bible icon. To set it, type `set preferred bible to` and the version name in the Command Box. Or drag that Bible to the top of your priority list.
- Your **Top Bibles** are the five Bibles highest in your priority list. Text Comparison, word studies and Bible searches can use them as a group.

For translation work, put the Bibles your team checks against most often in your top five.

## Make a collection

A **collection** is a named group of books, for example all your commentaries. You can search a collection or add it to a guide.

1. Open **Tools** and choose **Collections**.
2. Give the collection a name.
3. Type a **Rule**, for example `type:commentary`. Logos adds every book that matches.
4. Drag other books into **Plus these books**, or unwanted ones into **Minus these books**.

New books that match the rule join the collection by themselves. Collections only include books you have downloaded.

To search only a collection, click **Your Books** in Search and choose it under **Collections**.

## Translator's Workplace books

Faithlife's help pages say nothing about Translator's Workplace. TW books appear in your Library like any other books. Check in your own Library how they are listed and filtered.
