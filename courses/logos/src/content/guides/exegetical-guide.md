*Written from Faithlife's help pages for Logos version 55 (November 2026). Not yet checked in the app.*

## How it differs from the Passage Guide

The [Passage Guide](~/guides/passage-guide/) gives a broad overview of a passage. The **Exegetical Guide** looks at the words and phrases of the passage, especially the Greek and Hebrew words.

## Open it

1. Click **Guides / Workflows**, then **Exegetical Guide**.
2. Type the passage and press Enter.

For help on any section, point at its heading and click the **?** at the far right. This opens the matching help page.

## The main sections

- **Textual Variants:** links to textual commentaries, apparatuses and original-language texts for the passage.
- **Grammars:** articles from the grammars in your library, grouped by subject.
- **Grammatical Constructions:** point at a construction to see what it means. A "search for" link finds other places with the same construction.
- **Visualizations:** books that show the structure of the text.
- **Word by Word:** the grammar, form, meaning and syntax of each word, with links to lexicons.
- **Important Words:** the words that commentaries discuss most. Logos ranks them from all its commentaries, not only the ones you own.
- **Commentaries.**
- **Ancient Literature:** places where other ancient writings cite, quote or echo the passage.

The help page says the guide needs an internet connection. It is not clear whether that applies to the whole guide or only to Ancient Literature.

## What you will see

As with all guides, the sections and results depend on your Logos plan and the books in your library. Add, remove and move sections in the same way as in the Passage Guide.

## Using it in translation work

Start with **Word by Word** and **Textual Variants**. They show what is in the text and where the manuscripts differ. Read the commentaries after you have formed your own view.
