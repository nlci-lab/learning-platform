Use this form when you watch someone check a passage that can be read in more than one way, in a real session or in the [practice session](../../learn/checking-session/). It is for **feedback**. It is not a grade and it does not certify anyone.

Copy the form into your notes. Fill in what you saw, with a short example for each line.

## The form

**Passage:** ______  **Date:** ______

## The four levels

The levels describe what you saw in this session, not the person.

| Level | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 1 | Did not do this, or only after you prompted it. |
| 2 | Did it, but partly: for example, looked at one help only, or told the team the answer. |
| 3 | Did it well without help. |
| 4 | Did it well and helped the team learn to do it themselves. |

## Giving the feedback

- **Start with what you saw,** not with a judgement: "When the pastor said they followed the national Bible, you asked what the helps said."
- **Keep it to two points:** one that went well, one to try next time.
- **Let the person speak first:** "How do you think verse 49 went?"
- **Keep the form.** Over several sessions it shows growth, and the person may bring it to their own mentors in the programme.
