# Exegesis course: rules

These rules add to the root `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Subject:** exegesis for translation checking: finding what the original author meant, and judging whether a translation conveys it. For Bible translators and consultants.
- **Own words only.** Write in new words with new examples. Published works are cited, never copied.
- **Non-divisive examples.** Teaching examples use passages where the choice of meaning turns on language and context, not on doctrine that divides churches. Where a disputed passage cannot be avoided, the site sets out the options, the evidence and the reasons given for each, and does not choose.
- **Original languages.** Greek and Hebrew are shown in transliteration with a gloss; every claim about a form or a meaning names its source.
- **Scripture text:** NIV may be quoted with the notice in the footer; never ESV.
- **Levels:** Level 1 · First steps, Level 2 · Working level, Level 3 · Deeper study, Level 4 · Advanced (set in `course.config.ts`).
- **Checking cases** follow ADR 0006: constructed back translations, planted problems, review before publishing.
- **Generic audience:** no course, cohort, class or teacher is named on any page.
